## Stage 2: Scholars & Dreamers
**Type:** Trivia
1. Dr. Ratio often carries what object to signify his scholarly and somewhat condescending nature?
   **Answer:** A piece of chalk
2. What is Sunday's ultimate tragic goal in Penacony?
   **Answer:** To create a dream of perfect order without suffering
3. Who is the legendary, currently absent Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius?
   **Answer:** Varka

**Type:** Trivia
1. What is Yoichi Isagi's primary weapon on the field?
